## Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Intelligence System

**#MachineLearning #DataScience #Python #sklearn #Streamlit**

---

I'm excited to share my Week 12 Final Capstone Project: a complete, end-to-end Customer Churn Prediction system built from scratch in Python.

**The Business Problem:**
Telecommunications companies lose customers without advance warning. Every lost customer represents recurring revenue that's gone forever. I built a machine learning system to identify customers at risk of churning _before_ it happens — enabling proactive retention outreach.

**What I Built:**

✅ Full data science pipeline: validation → cleaning → EDA → feature engineering → preprocessing → modelling → evaluation → tuning → deployment

✅ 5 ML models trained and compared: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, HistGradientBoosting

✅ 9 business-driven engineered features including Customer Lifetime Value, Contract Risk score, and Charge-per-Tenure ratio

✅ Hyperparameter tuning with RandomizedSearchCV achieving **0.755 CV ROC-AUC**

✅ 12 EDA visualisation sets revealing key churn patterns (contract type, tenure, payment method)

✅ Customer risk scoring: 146 HIGH RISK / 145 MEDIUM RISK / 209 LOW RISK from 500 customers

✅ Streamlit web app with real-time prediction, probability gauge, and tailored retention recommendations

✅ 45/45 pytest tests passing across data, preprocessing, features, model, and deployment modules

**Tech Stack:**
Python | scikit-learn | Gradient Boosting | pandas | NumPy | matplotlib | seaborn | Streamlit | pytest | joblib | Jupyter

**Key Learnings:**
- Why **Recall > Accuracy** for imbalanced classification problems
- How to build **leakage-free** sklearn pipelines
- The difference between feature importance and causation
- How to translate ML outputs into **actionable business recommendations**
